For a club I can make everything a guest sees before the night, in the room and after it: from the announcement on social to the screens above the dance floor and the table booking on the site.

Before the night

  • Animated posters and line-up announcements in every vertical format: Reels, Stories, TikTok, VK Clips, Telegram. Example: EV Conference
  • Artist arrival teasers, countdowns, few-tables-left spots.
  • A weekly template, so each new party gets a new video in the same style, fast.
  • Ad variants for paid campaigns, to test which one works.
  • Videos for residents' and the label's releases, animated covers for streaming. Example: Theo (Mihigh Remix)

In the room

  • Loops for LED walls, projections and the DJ booth, at your screens' exact resolution, ready for Resolume (DXV, HAP, H.264). Example: Stage Visuals Pack
  • Visual packs for residents and theme nights, loops in time with the music. Example: BASSFACE
  • An artist intro, a midnight countdown, the club's own loops.
  • Greetings on the screens for VIP tables: a name and an occasion from a template, launched in seconds.
  • The club's animated logo and sound logo, one visual language for posters, screens, site and social.

After, and online

  • An aftermovie from your camera operator's footage or from phones: edit, cinematic colour and sound.
  • A week of short clips cut from one aftermovie.
  • A site: line-up, tickets, table booking on the floor plan, guest list, private events. Example: The Cozzi
  • A Telegram bot or one for your guests' messenger: bookings, guest list, announcements.
  • VIP invitations that open on the phone, a mini-game from a story link, a season-opening film in 3D and AI. Example: Wax-seal envelope

You can start small, with a weekly announcement template or a pack of loops for your screens. Results come fast, and you see how I work before anything bigger.

About

I started with a course for post-production super­vi­sors: run­ning edi­tors, sound design­ers and motion design­ers, and seeing where work was done at half effort. Then I spent two years as a film col­orist and stud­ied IT at Bauman in Moscow. I built my startup's app all the way to the App Store. Now I make films for clients abroad, and the site or app around them when they need one.

You get the whole cycle from one person: idea, design, ani­ma­tion, sound, edit, colour and, if needed, the site or app. When a project needs a senior designer, I work along­side Gio Kheladze, my studio part­ner.

How I work

  1. Brief A short call or chat: the task, the deadline, where it will be shown.
  2. Concept The idea, references, a storyboard or a prototype.
  3. Production Animation, 3D, sound, edit and colour, or the design and build of the site.
  4. Revisions You see interim versions; I take it to the finish.
  5. Delivery Every format you need: 9:16 for Reels and Stories, 4:5 and 1:1 for the feed, exact resolutions for your screens, source files by agreement.

Each stage is approved before the next. I name the price and timing after the brief, because they depend on the scope.
