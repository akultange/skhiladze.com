About

I started with a course for post-production super­vi­sors: run­ning edi­tors, sound design­ers and motion design­ers, and seeing where work was done at half effort. Then I spent two years as a film col­orist and stud­ied IT at Bauman in Moscow. I built my startup's app all the way to the App Store. Now I make films for clients abroad, and the site or app around them when they need one.

You get the whole cycle from one person: idea, design, ani­ma­tion, sound, edit, colour and, if needed, the site or app. When a project needs a senior designer, I work along­side Gio Kheladze, my studio part­ner.

How I work

  1. Brief A short call or chat: the task, the deadline, where it will be shown.
  2. Concept The idea, references, a storyboard or a prototype.
  3. Production Animation, 3D, sound, edit and colour, or the design and build of the site.
  4. Revisions You see interim versions; I take it to the finish.
  5. Delivery Every format you need: 9:16 for Reels and Stories, 4:5 and 1:1 for the feed, exact resolutions for your screens, source files by agreement.

Each stage is approved before the next. I name the price and timing after the brief, because they depend on the scope.

Tell me about the project. I'll answer with a plan and a price.
